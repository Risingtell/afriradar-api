# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else, is expected to be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind are not accepted.

Report a problem to **hello@afriradar.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block participants who break these rules.

This policy follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
